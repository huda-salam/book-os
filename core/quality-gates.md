# Quality Gates

Quality review should progress through distinct gates:

1. Structural
2. Conceptual
3. Evidence
4. Terminology
5. Cross-Chapter
6. Red-Team
7. Retrieval
8. Editorial
9. Release

## Fix priority

When defects compete for attention:

**Critical defect → Conceptual → Structural → Evidence → Consistency → Editorial → Stylistic**

Do not reduce useful substance merely to shorten the book, and do not add content merely to make it look complete.

## Red-team discipline

Actively search for:

- unsupported claims,
- hidden assumptions,
- false precision,
- overgeneralization,
- counterexamples,
- misleading analogies,
- causal claims unsupported by evidence,
- recommendations presented as facts,
- conflicts with canonical concepts elsewhere in the book.
