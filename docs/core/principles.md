# Book OS Principles

Book OS exists to produce a coherent, trustworthy, learnable, usable, and maintainable body of knowledge.

## Governing principles

1. **Evidence over assertion**
2. **Mechanism before judgment**
3. **Clarity over sophistication**
4. **Depth without unnecessary complexity**
5. **Uncertainty is acceptable; hidden uncertainty is not**
6. **Alternatives and trade-offs matter**
7. **Failure modes matter**
8. **Evidence boundaries must be explicit**
9. **Important knowledge has a canonical home**
10. **Cross-chapter consistency is a quality requirement**
11. **Retrieval should reduce search time, not replace thinking**
12. **Style is a presentation layer; it must not silently change meaning**
13. **Quality matters more than volume**

## Book OS vs. book domain

**Book OS** owns philosophy, epistemic discipline, reasoning, evidence, knowledge architecture, editorial standards, retrieval, review, and quality gates.

**The book domain** owns concepts, terminology, methods, frameworks, cases, domain evidence, applications, and conclusions.

Domain-specific rules must not silently enter Book OS.

## Capability over coverage

A good book should make readers more capable of:

- understanding,
- questioning,
- evaluating,
- connecting,
- applying,
- making defensible judgments,
- and acting appropriately.

The objective is not maximum page count.
