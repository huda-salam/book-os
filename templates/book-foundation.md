- [ ] Thesis is clear

- [ ] Conceptual model is coherent
- [ ] Candidate architecture is justified
- [ ] Canonical terminology is identified
- [ ] Evidence strategy exists
- [ ] Quality strategy exists
- [ ] Retrieval strategy exists
- [ ] Major unknowns are explicit
